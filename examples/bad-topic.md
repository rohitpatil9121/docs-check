# Restore a file

## Before you begin

You need access to the kubernetes cluster and a login to the dashboard.

#### Steps

1. Simply click on **Restore** in order to open the restore wizard, then pick the snapshot you want, choose the destination folder, confirm the overwrite settings, and then wait for the restore job to finish.
2. See [Snapshot policies](policies.md) and [Retention](#retention-rules).
